To Whom It May Concern,

Recently, I conducted a litter audit for the Moonee Ponds Creek, which involved a walk around Flemington Bridge. During my walk, I noticed a distinct lack of easily accessible bins excluding park area. While there are some bins available at the tram stations, they are primarily located in the middle of laneways and not along the footpaths where pedestrians are most likely to need them.

This is a substantial problem, as it has led to increased littering in the area. Without conveniently located bins, individuals may be less inclined to dispose of their waste responsibly. The absence of accessible waste disposal affects your ability to keep the Moonee Ponds creek clean and all the other initiatives you have to clean up the pond chain. This leads to options not only impacts the environment.

I believe that addressing this issue by installing more bins along the footpaths could significantly improve waste management and lower littering rates.

Thank you for reading.

Best regards,
Matthew Freriks
